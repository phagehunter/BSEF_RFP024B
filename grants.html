Request for Proposals

Rapid Diagnostics in Rural and Remote Regions

Overview

There is a critical lack of access to emerging healthcare technologies to address rapid diagnostic testing for refugee and displaced populations, particularly in rural or remote areas. According to the WHO, 1 in 8 people are currently on the move globally, and host communities are often unequipped to meet their unique health needs.

The Bleeding Edge Scientific Foundation (BESF) aims to accelerate the deployment of new technologies to prevent disease in these vulnerable populations and promote global health security.

Technical Objectives

  • API Integration: Proposals must demonstrate standardized real-time data sharing with global health security networks.
  • LMIC Manufacturing: Priority is given to sustainable production models within Low- and Middle-Income Countries.
  • 7-1-7 Framework: Alignment with the 7-1-7 framework for detection and response is mandatory.

Eligibility & Guidance

This RFP is open to non-profits, academic institutions, and technical partners. We prioritize projects that demonstrate equitable access for 501(c)(3) organizations and humanitarian groups.

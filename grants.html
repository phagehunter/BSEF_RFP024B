Request for Proposals

Rapid Diagnostics in Rural and Remote Regions

Overview

The Bleeding Edge Scientific Foundation (BESF) is addressing the critical lack of access to emerging healthcare technologies for rapid diagnostic testing among refugee and displaced populations.

As 1 in 8 people are currently on the move globally, host communities are often unequipped to meet these specific health needs. This award aims to increase the availability of tech in remote areas.

Technical Objectives

  • API Integration: Use standardized APIs for real-time data sharing with health security networks.
  • LMIC Manufacturing: Priority for manufacturing models within Low- and Middle-Income Countries.
  • 7-1-7 Framework: Alignment with the 7-1-7 response framework is mandatory.
